Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23).

`main.py` writes one sentence pitching the worst, most useless app
imaginable to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, stdlib-only, no network.

The pitch: DoorbellDJ composes a unique 45-second orchestral fanfare every
time someone rings your doorbell — because no guest should ever arrive
without a grand entrance.
